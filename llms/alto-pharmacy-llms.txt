# Alto Pharmacy

> Alto Pharmacy is a San Francisco digital pharmacy and pharmacy-technology company
> (founded 2015 as ScriptDash). It runs a licensed full-service pharmacy on its own
> pharmacy operating system, Alto OS, delivering retail, specialty and fertility
> prescriptions with same-day courier delivery, pharmacist care and automated benefits
> investigation — and it licenses that stack to pharmaceutical manufacturers, health
> plans, health systems and digital health companies as Alto Hub+, Alto Dispensing and
> Alto Complete.

**API status: no public API.** Alto markets "intake and transfer APIs", "API integrations"
and "Surescripts or transfer API intake" to partners, but publishes no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDK and no sandbox. Technical
access is arranged through the partnerships team (partnerships@alto.com). Probes of
api.alto.com, www.alto.com, providers.alto.com and app.alto.com found no machine-readable
contract and no /.well-known discovery document as of 2026-08-06.
